## Reasons you should switch

- You can spend years customizing your system and still find new ways to tweak it
- It can be enjoyed by minimalists and power users alike
- Bloatware *doesn't exist* (because you build it from the ground up)
- It teaches you exactly how a Linux environment actually works
- Heck even [PEWDIEPIE uses arch](https://youtu.be/pVI_smLgTY0)

Front-end Development is the Worst
