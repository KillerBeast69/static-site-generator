## Introduction

Let's be completely honest about the state of web development today. When you strip away the flashy frameworks, it's really just a bunch of `div`s and `span`s, right? And CSS? Don't even get me started. It's a constant, agonizing battle of, "Oh, I want this to be red, but not *thaaaaat* red." 

What a joke.

## The Illusion of Complexity

### Silly Markup Languages

People try to dress up front-end work as complex engineering, but we all know the truth. Real programmers write actual code; they don't spend their days wrestling with silly markup languages just trying to center a button on a screen. 

## The Path of the True Programmer

### Embrace the Terminal

If you want to do actual engineering, you need to strip away the graphical training wheels and use the right tools:

* **The OS:** Real programmers code on Arch Linux, not macOS, and certainly not Windows.

* **The Editor:** They use Vim, not bloated memory-hogs like VS Code.

* **The Language:** They use C, not HTML.

## Conclusion

Stop playing around in the browser inspector and pretending it's computer science. It's time to leave the browser behind. Come to the [backend](https://www.boot.dev/dashboard), where the real programming happens.
